# Optional fabrication

Capabilities checked against provider pages on **18 November 2026**. The workshop audience requested a few pieces with US/EU delivery. A particular destination, design, material, quantity, taxes, finish and delivery date still need a current quote. Do not reuse promotional starting prices as a prototype budget.

These services are candidates for US/EU delivery, not confirmed quotes for a specific address. Compare offers using the same model, quantity, process, finish and destination.

## What to send for a printed object

Keep the editable CAD source; export each separately printed part as STEP and/or STL in millimetres. Include a preview, intended use, critical dimensions and fit surfaces, material/finish preferences, quantity and destination. Ask the supplier which minimum walls, clearances and finishing allowances apply to the selected process. A validated solid can still violate a particular process's rules.

For an enclosure, request a first fit sample before committing to several finished gifts. A personalized mechanical object may need only printing. A device built around off-the-shelf modules additionally needs the modules, wiring, firmware and final assembly.

## An instant quote is also a check

With the person's approval for the upload, an instant-quote page answers several questions before any order. Exercised on Craftcloud on 18 November 2026, for one object of five parts and about 64 cm³, delivered to the United States. The object was the 80 mm ball-shaped shell described in [printable sample](printable-sample.md), not the desk timer in this kit:

- The page asked for location, currency and the unit of the files. Its confirm button also accepted the service's terms of use; treat that as a separate consent.
- It showed the size and volume of each file. Compare them with the CAD report: a unit error shows here.
- No account was needed to see materials, prices and delivery dates.
- Each material card stated an accuracy and a minimum wall. Read on that day: SLS Nylon PA12 and MJF Nylon PA12, ±0.3% with a lower limit of ±0.3 mm, wall 0.8 mm; PETG by FDM, ±0.3% with a lower limit of ±0.2 mm, wall 1 mm; standard and tough resin, ±0.2% with a lower limit of ±0.2 mm, wall 0.6-1 mm.
- Best offers for that object, parts plus shipping: PETG $26.79 + $6.00; standard resin $69.80 + $10.00; SLS Nylon PA12 $75.04 + $8.00; MJF Nylon PA12 $78.75 + $23.09; tough resin $90.24 + $4.00. Delivery was shown as starting two to five days later. These are the numbers of one object on one day, before finish, colour and tax: an order of magnitude, not a budget.

A quote means that the files parsed and a price exists. It is not a manufacturability review: the page priced a version whose clearances were smaller than the stated accuracy, and gave no warning.

## What to send for custom electronics

PCBWay lists Gerber fabrication files, BOM, pick-and-place data, enclosure files and assembly instructions for a complete box-build quote. Ask explicitly whether firmware loading, functional testing and the requested quantity are included. A compiled Zener file or an unrouted KiCad board is not this package.

## A gift or a shared batch

If the maker wants a gift, add recipient-specific size, texture, colors or personalization to their concept. Do not replace the idea with a generic holiday gadget. For December 25, work backward from the supplier's quoted arrival date and leave room for a first sample and revision. No Christmas cutoff or delivery commitment was verified in this research.

Prepare a quote request as text with the file list. Uploading files, contacting suppliers, ordering and paying are separate actions requiring authorization. A shared class order is a possibility for later, not part of completing the workshop.
