# Redesign round

Use one prompt that puts pressure on a real property of the chosen object. Let the participant pick another if it destroys the purpose or creates a different product. Keep the original artifact and make one revision.

| Card | Try | What could reveal a problem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smaller bag | Reduce one outside dimension by 20%. | Component containment, wall thickness, connector access. |
| One free hand | The user must hold or operate it with one hand. | Reach, grip, button force assumptions, where it rests. |
| Quiet room | It must communicate without sound. | How the user notices and interprets the output. |
| Shared object | Another person must understand the first action without an explanation. | A peer's interpretation of the controls and preview. |
| Cable reality | The cable needs room to enter and bend. | A plug envelope and clearance, not just a hole for the socket. |
| One fewer part | Remove a component or combine two functions. | The effect on interaction, assembly, or reliability. |

Do not score an imagined user's behavior as measured usability. For a digital prototype, describe the question for a later physical trial.

For a size change, point to X/Y/Z first. After the failed check, distinguish a placement that did not move with the wall from a fundamental lack of room. Keep the failed variant marked "do not print" and report the remaining space after repair.

At the gallery, show the object first. A peer guesses what to do with it, then the inventor shows the intended interaction and the revision. A surprising mismatch is useful, not a failed presentation. Optional votes: "I would use this", "I would give this to someone", and "best rethink after the constraint". No business pitch is required.
